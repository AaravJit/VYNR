# Licence for the vehicle specification data

`data/specs.json` and the file generated from it, `src/catalog/specs.data.ts`, are adapted from English Wikipedia articles written by Wikipedia contributors. Each record lists the articles it came from under `sources`. The articles were accessed on 25 November 2026.

**Changes made:** figures and option names were extracted from the articles and restructured into one record per vehicle generation. Nothing was estimated, converted or averaged.
